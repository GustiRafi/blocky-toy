# Flexible Script Input Template

Scripts supplied to `/blocky-toy --script <path>` do NOT need strict formatting. Any combination of headings, stage directions, speaker lines, and cues works:

```markdown

Language: en-US | Duration: 35s | Format: 16:9

## Scene 1 — EXT. SUBURBAN TRAIN PLATFORM - NIGHT (LIGHT RAIN)
- Camera: Wide establishing shot along wet platform edge under station awning.
- Weather/SFX: Steady light rain on canopy, distant low train horn.

LEO stands beneath the shelter checking his watch, raindrops glistening on dark asphalt.
MINA runs up slightly breathless, holding a folded yellow ticket.

MINA (relieved, catching breath): Wait—did I miss the 9:15?
LEO (warm, checking schedule board): No. Five minutes late because of the rain.

[SFX: Soft station chime overhead.]
[CAMERA: Medium two-shot; both turn toward approaching headlight glow off-screen left.]
```
